Daily Reading Tips

  1. Set a daily 20-minute reading goal.
  2. Keep a small “to-read” list — no more than 5 titles.
  3. Alternate between fiction and non-fiction.
  4. Write a 3-line summary after each chapter.
  5. Read at the same time every day — habit stacks faster.
